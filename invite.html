Why this is not just a WhatsApp message

You will see who they are. Every runner on Relay has had their government ID checked against a photograph of their face, by a person. You are not taking anybody's word for it.

You pay them directly. Cash, EcoCash, however the two of you agree. The money never passes through Relay, so we cannot hold it up or take a cut of it.

If it goes wrong, there is proof. What was agreed, when each step happened, what was said, and photographs at collection and handover — kept permanently. That is what an argument three months later gets decided on.

Nothing is agreed by opening this. You will see the errand and what it costs first, and you can change the amount if the two of you remember it differently.

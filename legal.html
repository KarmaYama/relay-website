Terms of Service

Closed Beta • Last Updated: September 2026

The short version. Relay introduces you to verified people and keeps a permanent record of what happens. Relay never holds, moves or touches your money — you pay the runner directly, between yourselves. If something goes wrong we will investigate with real evidence and real consequences, but we are not your bank, not your insurer, and we cannot reverse a payment we never held.

1. What Relay is

Relay ("the Platform") is a technology service that connects independent Runners with Clients, verifies their identities, and records what happens on each job. Relay is not a transportation carrier, a courier company, a logistics provider, a bank, a payment processor, an escrow agent, or an insurer.

Runners are independent people, not employees, agents or contractors of Relay. Relay does not direct how, when or by what route a Runner performs a job.

2. Payments — Relay holds no funds

This is the most important term in this document, so it is stated plainly:

  • Relay never receives, holds, escrows, routes or releases the money for a job. The price shown in the app is an amount you and the other party have agreed between yourselves.
  • You pay the Runner directly — in cash, by mobile money, by bank transfer, or by whatever method the two of you agree. That transaction happens entirely outside Relay.
  • Relay does not charge your card for a job, does not "lock" or reserve your funds, and cannot freeze, reverse, refund or claw back a payment. We have no ability to do so, because the money never passes through us.
  • When a Runner marks a job as paid, that is their claim, timestamped and recorded. It is not Relay confirming that money changed hands. Only your own confirmation does that.

Relay's own fee. Relay charges a small flat matching fee when a job is accepted by a Runner. That fee is the only money Relay ever collects, it is separate from what you pay the Runner, and it is not a percentage of the job.

We store no card numbers and no bank account details, because we never need them.

3. Identity and verification

You must provide accurate, legal identity information — your name, date of birth and gender as they appear on your government-issued ID. Runners must complete identity verification, including an ID document and a selfie, before accepting any job.

Providing false identity information, using someone else's identity, or holding more than one account is a serious breach of these terms and will result in immediate suspension.

Verification means we have checked that a real, identifiable person is behind an account. It is not a character reference, a background check, a criminal-record check, or a guarantee of anyone's honesty or competence.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Agree the price and the payment method clearly, before the job starts.
  • Use the in-app chat for anything that matters. If it is not on the record, we cannot use it as evidence later.
  • Confirm completion honestly, and promptly, once you have received what you agreed.
  • Do not use Relay to arrange anything unlawful.

5. Disputes

Either party may raise a dispute on a job that is in progress, where payment has been claimed, or within 48 hours of completion. Raising a dispute requires selecting a reason and describing what happened; you may attach photographs as evidence.

The other party then has 24 hours to respond with their own account and evidence. Not responding is treated as an adverse signal, but is not by itself treated as proof — a human always reviews before any determination is made.

Relay's Trust & Safety team reviews the job record: timestamps, GPS check-ins, chat history and any evidence submitted by either side. Possible outcomes are:

  • the dispute is dismissed as unsubstantiated;
  • a warning is recorded against an account;
  • an account is suspended pending further investigation;
  • an account is permanently banned, and the associated identity document is blacklisted to prevent re-registration;
  • the matter is escalated to law enforcement.

What a determination does and does not do. It records a finding against a verified identity, and it may end someone's ability to use Relay. It does not move money, order anyone to pay, or compensate a loss — Relay has no power to do any of those things. Relay's determination is not an arbitration award and does not replace your right to pursue the other party directly or through the courts.

6. Evidence and police referral

Where a dispute involving theft, fraud or non-delivery is substantiated, Relay can compile an incident packet — both parties' verified identity details, the full job record, timestamps, GPS data, chat history and photographic evidence — formatted to be handed to a police officer or attached to an affidavit.

You may request your own incident packet and take it to the police yourself at any time. In serious cases, or where there is a pattern of substantiated findings against one identity, Relay may approach law enforcement directly. Relay does not file police reports automatically or on the basis of unverified claims.

7. Records are permanent

Job records, disputes and verification history are retained permanently and cannot be deleted, including by you, and including after you close your account. This is what makes reputation on Relay meaningful. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.

8. Prohibited use

You may not use Relay to transport or arrange: illegal substances, weapons, hazardous materials, large sums of cash, live animals, stolen goods, or anything whose transport requires a licence you do not hold. You may not use Relay to harass, threaten, defraud or endanger anyone.

9. Suspension and termination

We may suspend or permanently close an account for breach of these terms, for substantiated fraud or theft, for a safety concern, or where we are legally required to. Where an account is closed for substantiated fraud, a permanent mark is recorded against that verified identity and their ID document is blacklisted against re-registration.

You may close your account at any time. Closing it does not remove your job history.

10. Limitation of liability

By using Relay you acknowledge that:

  • Runners are independent freelancers, not employees of Relay;
  • Relay provides identity verification, record-keeping and dispute review — it does not insure goods, guarantee performance, or guarantee payment;
  • Relay is not liable for goods lost, stolen, damaged or delayed during a job;
  • Relay is not liable for a party failing to pay, since Relay never held the money;
  • to the maximum extent permitted by law, Relay's total liability to you for any claim is limited to the matching fees you have paid to Relay in the six months preceding the claim.

This is closed beta software provided "as is". It will have defects. Do not rely on it for anything where failure would cause you serious harm or loss.

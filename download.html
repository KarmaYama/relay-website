View Changelog

Relay looks like Relay now. The app has been rebuilt on a single, shared visual language: proper typography, softer corners, and a calmer palette in place of hard black on hard white. This is the first version where the app's intended lettering actually appears on your screen at all.

Your email address and phone number can both be changed, from your profile. Changing either one asks you to confirm it again, because a number or address you have just swapped is not one you have proven.

Your profile now tells you the truth about what is verified. It used to guess, and could show a phone number as confirmed when it never had been.

A What's new entry now lives in your account menu, so you can read this again whenever you like rather than catching it once.

Relay never holds your money

You pay your runner directly. There is no wallet, no escrow and no held balance. What Relay gives you is a verified identity on the other side of the job, a permanent timestamped record of what happened, and a dispute process staffed by people.

Seeing “Can't reach Relay”?

Turn off any VPN, particularly one that blocks ads. Those filter DNS for your whole phone, while your browser quietly routes around them — so this site loads normally and the app still cannot connect. Also set Private DNS to Automatic, or try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
